Troubleshooting note: intermittent connectivity failures on Catalyst edge platforms are commonly associated with WAN interface errors, SD-WAN policy misconfiguration, power supply redundancy loss, or overheating. Recommended diagnostics before ordering parts: check interface error counters, review SD-WAN Analytics/ThousandEyes for path health, verify dual power supply status, and confirm the software version against Cisco field notices.
