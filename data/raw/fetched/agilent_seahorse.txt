Agilent Seahorse Real-Time Cell Metabolic Analysis
Source: https://www.agilent.com/en/product/cell-analysis/real-time-cell-metabolic-analysis

With over 20,000 genes, 200,000 proteins and thousands of pathways, you can't measure everything in a cell at once, but you can measure the energy that drives them.

Agilent offers cell analysis technology solutions that detect discrete changes in cellular bioenergetics in real time when using 2D and 3D cell models. Real-time cell measurements provide a clear window into the critical functions driving cell signaling, proliferation, activation, toxicity and biosynthesis.

Products:
- XF Analyzers: Agilent Seahorse XF Analyzers provide metabolic data on energy production in live cells in real time.
- XF Software: Agilent XF cell analysis software for assay design, data acquisition, analysis, and reporting.
- Plate Reader Metabolic Assays: easy-to-use kits and reagents for measuring real-time energy flux, mitochondrial function, glycolysis and substrate utilization in living cells, isolated mitochondria, 3D cultures and microbes.
