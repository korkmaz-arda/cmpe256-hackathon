Agilent Artisan Link Pro for Special Stains
Source: https://www.agilent.com/en/product/special-stains

Artisan Link Pro. The consistent, safe and easy choice for special stains. The world's first advanced, fully automated solution for special stains, the Artisan Link Pro Special Staining System helps pathology labs enhance performance and deliver accurate results for diagnostic confidence.

It is a unique, complete solution optimized for special histochemical staining. With an intuitive user interface and integrated software, it automates the special staining process from drying and deparaffinization through to finished slides.

The extensive portfolio of ready-to-use Artisan special stains kits and preoptimized, editable protocols enables laboratories to automate even complex special stains with minimal user interaction, delivering consistent, clear staining every time.

The solution combines the Special Staining System, ready-to-use special stains kits, accessories, a computer with DakoLink software, report printer, slide label printer, and Premium Service & Support.
