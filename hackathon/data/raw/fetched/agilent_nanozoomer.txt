Agilent NanoZoomer Slide Scanner System (Digital Pathology)
Source: https://www.agilent.com/en/product/digital-pathology/digital-pathology-scanners/nanozoomer-slide-scanner-system-4370282

NanoZoomer 360MD Slide Scanner System is a whole slide scanner that converts glass slides into high-resolution digital data by high-speed scanning for primary diagnostic use (IVD). Pathologists are aided in viewing and diagnosis of anatomical histopathology slides by its high-throughput automated digital slide creation, viewing, and management system.

The scanner has automated tissue detection and automated scanning for routine applications. Scanning can be done at 40x or 20x with standard glass slides (27mm/76mm), allowing file-size management. Image resolution of 0.23 micrometer/pixel at 40x and 0.46 micrometer/pixel at 20x. Z-stacking capabilities on all scanners allow focusing at different sample depths.

Features: engineered for digitizing routine clinical pathology slides; open, fully automated system with flexible scanning for clear images even in complex tissues; flexible scanning management allowing prioritization of urgent requests; 20x objective with precision optical coupler for 20x/40x scanning.

Agilent digital pathology ecosystem spans staining to AI decision support.
